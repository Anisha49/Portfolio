My approach

01 — UNDERSTAND
Start with people

I spend more time understanding the problem than designing the solution. Surveys, interviews, competitive analysis — all before Figma opens.

03 — DESIGN
Prove it, then build it

Every design decision I make has a reason behind it. I test, iterate, and refine until the solution earns its place.

02 — QUESTION
Find the real problem

Good design starts with the right problem. I ask uncomfortable questions early so we're not solving the wrong thing beautifully.

04 — DELIVER
Design that ships

I work within constraints — brand systems, stakeholders, live products. I make meaningful changes without blowing up what exists.
